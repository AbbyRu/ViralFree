These strategies drive organic TikTok growth — no paid boosts. The algorithm rewards engagement signals, not just spend.

Hook in 0–2 seconds
TikTok decides to push or kill your video in the first 2 seconds. Open with a bold visual or a statement that forces curiosity.
Before & after flips
Transformation videos get rewatched 3–5x on average. Show the thrifted item, the flip, and final look back-to-back.
Take people to the market
Market POVs perform incredibly well — the noise, crowds, and finds make viewers feel they're discovering treasure with you.
Comment-bait endings
End with "comment your size and I'll DM you" or "which piece should I keep?" Comments signal quality to the algorithm.
Post at peak times
For East Africa, best windows are 7–9am, 12–2pm, and 8–10pm EAT. Consistency beats frequency — post at the same time daily.
Use niche hashtags only
#thriftkenya #gikombafashion #nairobishop outperform #fyp massively. Use 3–5 specific tags your target buyers actually follow.
Duet & stitch creators
Duetting a video with 100k+ views injects your content into an existing engaged audience. Add your unique reaction or take.
Jump on trending sounds early
Use trending audio within its first 24–48 hours for maximum algorithmic lift. Check TikTok's trending sound shelf daily.
Reply to every comment
Comment activity is a major signal. Video replies to comments re-enter your video into the distribution cycle automatically.
